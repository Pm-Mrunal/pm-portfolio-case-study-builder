# Recruiter TLDR — Output Template

> Format: Five labeled sub-blocks. Not a prose essay with bullets appended — five distinct, named sections.
> Target: 350-500 words of content (not counting headers or dividers).
> Optimized for fast scanning. The reader should know context, problem, stakes, ownership, and results within 45 seconds.
> Used by `/generate` as structural guidance.

---

[Project Title]
[Company] | [Role] | [Timeline] | [Stage: Shipped / Pilot / Pre-launch]

---

At a glance
[One sentence. Set the company and product context, then name the project. Do not start with "I." Do not repeat the project title verbatim. Example: "Vantage is a B2B SaaS spend management platform. This project redesigned the self-serve onboarding flow to remove friction at the critical activation step, increasing 7-day activation by 22%, reducing CS onboarding calls by 40%, and opening the SMB segment without adding headcount."]

---

The Problem
[2-3 sentences. Name: who experienced the problem, what the prior state was, and what the business stakes were. Lead with the problem — not the company, not a summary. Make the reader feel the weight of what was broken before the Why This Matters sub-block explains the stakes.]

---

Why This Matters
[1-2 sentences. The structural or strategic reason this problem was worth solving — not just the symptoms. This is the "so what" for a reader evaluating PM judgment. Should read as insight, not recap. Example: "The separation wasn't just inefficient — it was blocking price-sensitive customers from accessing a service they needed and creating structural debt the business couldn't collect."]

---

What I Owned
1. [PM action phrase beginning with a verb — Led / Defined / Designed / Cut / Ran / Drove / Partnered with / Established]
2. [Another specific ownership area]
3. [Another]
4. [Another]
5. [Another]
6. [Optional sixth item — only if genuinely distinct from above]
7. [Optional seventh item]

[Numbered items only — no bullet points for this block. Only use "Led" or "Owned" if source data explicitly states first-person leadership. Default to "Contributed to," "Partnered with," or "Supported" where ownership is shared or unclear.]

---

Key Results
- [Most compelling quantitative result — exact numbers from source data, no rounding or approximation]
- [Second result — a different dimension from the first: user, operational, financial, or behavioral]
- [Third result]
- [Fourth result if available]
- [Optional fifth — the most surprising or counterintuitive result if one exists in the source data]

[Do not introduce results that are not in the source data. If hard metrics are absent, use directional signals phrased precisely: "bad debt eliminated for portal Standard bookings" not "bad debt significantly reduced." End with the most unexpected or counterintuitive outcome if one exists.]

---

> Generator notes — do not include in output:
> - No em dashes anywhere
> - No bold markdown in any sub-block prose
> - If role ownership used team language in source docs, use "contributed to" throughout — never upgrade to "led" or "owned"
> - If project is pre-launch or pilot, state it explicitly in the title block and Key Results
> - "Why This Matters" should feel like a structural insight, not a restatement of the problem
> - "What I Owned" must be numbered items, not bullet points
